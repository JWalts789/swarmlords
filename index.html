HOW TO PLAY

THE GARDEN. A fresh map every campaign. You and three rival kingdoms hold territories; the wilds hold the rest. Rivals war with each other as much as with you. Last kingdom standing wins.

YOUR TURN. Collect gold, then take ONE action: ATTACK a neighboring territory, FORTIFY a garrison, or WAIT for +2 gold. The shop at your capital is always open on your turn.

BATTLE. Tap a card, tap a lane. Bugs march up and fight what they meet. Chew the enemy hive to zero. Fliers skip past ground troops but fall to anything that shoots up — only heavy fliers that STRAFE will fight the ground. Enemy garrisons are finite; outlast their reserves and the field is yours.

LOSING. Lose a battle, lose that territory — not the run. You're only done when you hold nothing at all.

GROWING. Conquer a territory to recruit from its native species and collect its yield every turn. Plan a campaign trail toward the boons you want.

LOYALTY. Keep your deck at least half your own kind and your faction's units fight harder (KINDRED). At 75% you are DEVOTED and unlock your kingdom's loyalty power. Rival armies are always devoted — off-faction recruits are strong, but they dilute you.
